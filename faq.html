Support Center

Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to the most common questions across all topics.

General Inquiry

What is ByWith?

ByWith is a comprehensive digital infrastructure provider for modern businesses.

Is there a free trial?

Yes, 14-day free trial on all plans.

How do I reset my password?

You can reset your password from the login page by clicking "Forgot Password".

Billing & Payment

What payment methods are accepted?

We accept Visa, Mastercard, AMEX, PayPal, Apple Pay, Google Pay, and bank transfers for enterprise customers.

Can I get a refund?

Yes, we offer a 30-day money-back guarantee on all purchases. Contact support to initiate a refund.

How do I update my billing information?

You can update billing details in your account dashboard under Settings > Billing.

Are there any hidden fees?

No, all pricing is transparent. The price you see is what you pay, with no hidden fees.

Shipping & Delivery

What are the shipping options?

We offer standard (3-5 days), express (1-2 days), and same-day delivery in select cities.

Do you ship internationally?

Yes, we ship to over 50 countries worldwide. Use our shipping availability checker to confirm delivery to your location.

Can I track my order?

Yes, you'll receive a tracking number via email once your order ships. Track it in your dashboard or carrier website.

What if my package is lost or damaged?

Contact our support team immediately. We'll investigate and arrange a replacement or full refund.

Products & Services

Do you offer custom solutions?

Yes, we provide customized enterprise solutions tailored to your business needs. Contact sales for consultation.

What is your warranty policy?

All hardware products come with a 1-year manufacturer warranty. Software products include lifetime updates.

Can I upgrade or downgrade my plan?

Yes, you can change your subscription plan anytime from your dashboard. Changes take effect immediately.

Do you offer training or onboarding?

Yes, we provide comprehensive training sessions and documentation for all enterprise customers.

Account & Security

How do I secure my account?

Enable two-factor authentication (2FA) in your security settings and use a strong, unique password.

Can I delete my account?

Yes, go to Settings > Account > Delete Account. Note that this action is permanent and cannot be undone.

Is my data encrypted?

Yes, all data is encrypted with 256-bit SSL encryption both in transit and at rest.

What happens if I forget my password?

Click "Forgot Password" on the login page and follow the instructions sent to your registered email.

Technical Support

How do I contact support?

Email us at support@bywith.com, use live chat on our website, or call our 24/7 hotline at 1-800-BYWITH.

What are your support hours?

We offer 24/7 support for all customers via email and chat. Phone support is available Mon-Fri 9 AM - 6 PM EST.

Do you have a knowledge base?

Yes, visit our Help Center at help.bywith.com for tutorials, guides, and troubleshooting articles.

Can I request a new feature?

Absolutely! Submit feature requests through your dashboard or email them to features@bywith.com.
